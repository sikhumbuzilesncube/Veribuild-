Frequently Asked Questions

What is a BOQ?

A Bill of Quantities (BOQ) is a detailed list of all materials needed for your building project, with quantities and costs.

Do I need to sign up to generate a BOQ?

Yes, you need to sign up (free) to generate a BOQ. Sign up takes less than 1 minute.

Can I take photos instead of scanning?

Yes! You can take photos of your plan using your phone camera. We'll convert them to a PDF automatically.

How do I find a skilled worker?

Click the "Find a Worker" button to browse plumbers, electricians, painters, and other tradespeople near you.

How do skilled workers get jobs?

Workers appear in BOQ recommendations and receive emergency job alerts based on their location and trade.

Do you offer commercial BOQs?

Not yet. Commercial BOQs (offices, shops, schools) are coming soon.
