🏗️ About VeriBuild

Built with ❤️ from Zimbabwe for the African market.

VeriBuild is a technology platform that connects clients with building professionals, hardware shops, and structural engineers across Africa.

Who We Are

VeriBuild is a technology platform built with ❤️ from Zimbabwe for the African market. We are a product of Gatekeeper AI — a Zimbabwean technology company focused on solving real problems through innovation.

Our Mission

To make building easier, more transparent, and more affordable for everyone across Africa.

What We Do

  • BOQ Generation: Upload your building plan and get a detailed Bill of Quantities with material costs from local hardware shops.
  • Find Skilled Workers: Browse verified plumbers, electricians, painters, bricklayers, and more.
  • Hardware Price Comparison: Compare prices from local hardware shops to get the best deals.
  • Structural Engineers: Connect with registered structural engineers for complex projects.

Why Choose VeriBuild

  • African: Built for African markets by Africans.
  • Transparent: No hidden fees. You pay only for what you use.
  • Trusted: All service providers are verified and rated by real clients.
  • Affordable: Save money on Quantity Surveyor fees and material costs.

Our Values

  • Integrity: We do what we say.
  • Transparency: Clear pricing and honest communication.
  • Community: We support local businesses and workers.
  • Innovation: We use technology to solve real problems.
