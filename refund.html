💰 Refund Policy

Last Updated: 19 August 2026

⚠️ Important: Please read this refund policy carefully before making any payments on VeriBuild.

1. Introduction

VeriBuild — a product of Gatekeeper AI — provides digital services including Bill of Quantities (BOQ) generation, platform subscriptions, and referral commissions. This policy outlines when and how refunds are issued.

2. BOQ Services

❌ Non-Refundable: BOQ fees are non-refundable once the report has been generated and delivered.

Exceptions: Refunds may be considered in the following cases:

  • Double Payment: If you accidentally paid twice for the same BOQ
  • Technical Error: If our system failed to generate a valid BOQ due to a technical issue
  • Incorrect Charge: If you were charged the wrong amount

Refund Process: Submit a refund request through our Contact Us page within 7 days of payment. Provide your order reference and reason for refund.

3. Subscriptions

❌ Non-Refundable: All subscription fees (Hardware, Worker Premium, Engineer) are non-refundable.

Why? Subscriptions provide immediate access to premium features. We do not offer partial refunds for unused time.

Cancellation: You can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. You will continue to have access to premium features until the end of your paid period.

4. Referral Commissions

⚠️ Commission Policy: Referral commissions are earned when a referred client completes a BOQ payment.
  • Commissions are calculated automatically by our system
  • Commissions are available for withdrawal once they reach the minimum threshold
  • Commissions are reversed if the referred client's payment is refunded
  • No refunds are issued for commissions already withdrawn

5. Payment Processing Fees

Payment processing fees charged by Paynow are non-refundable. If you receive a refund, the processing fee will be deducted from your refund amount.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please:

  • Step 1: Log in to your VeriBuild account
  • Step 2: Go to your dashboard → Support → Refund Request
  • Step 3: Provide your order reference, payment date, and reason
  • Step 4: Wait for our team to review your request (1-3 business days)
  • Step 5: If approved, refunds are processed within 7-10 business days

7. Refund Timeline

  • EcoCash: Refunds are sent to your EcoCash number within 24 hours of approval
  • Bank Transfers: Refunds to bank accounts take 3-5 business days
  • Card Payments: Refunds to cards take 5-10 business days

8. Disputes

If you have a dispute regarding a payment or refund, please contact us at admin@gatekeeperai.co.zw or call +263 77 780 3517. We will work to resolve your issue promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or platform notification.
